# Blinkit Sales Dashboard - Power BI Project

![blinkit]9Blinkit%20Sales%20Report.PNG)

## Project Overview

This **Power BI Dashboard** provides a complete analytical view of a **Blinkit Sales performance** across multiple dimensions — including **Outlet Size, Outlet Location, City, various Product categories, Customer Spending Behavior, and Time-Based Trends**.

The goal of this report is to help the management team make **data-driven decisions** by tracking key KPIs such as **Outlet Size, Outlet Location, Outlet Type, Top Selling products & Fat items based on total sales, Avg Sales, No. items sold, and Average rating.**

----

## Business Objectives

- Analyze **Yearly sales trends** for better forecasting.  
- Identify **top-selling Items** and **Fat Content** for maximum profit.  
- Compare **outlet size Vs Outlet location** to plan promotions effectively.  
- Evaluate Sales on **outlet Type** 
- Provide **insights into sales performance** over time for business growth.
